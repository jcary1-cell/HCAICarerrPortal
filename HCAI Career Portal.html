        Read like a recruiter

        How to read a posting

        Green lights

        • "All majors" or "no specific major required"
        • "Strong writing skills"
        • "Policy, ethics, or law"
        • Research, synthesis, communication, stakeholder work

        Skip unless you have it

        • Python or statistics required
        • A design portfolio required
        • "Past posting" doesn't mean closed for good. These usually repeat each year, so set a reminder.
